Before you apply

Adoption information

Adopting an animal is a long-term commitment. Please review the animal's listed diet and minimum enclosure needs and make sure you are prepared to provide appropriate ongoing care.

01

Choose an animal

Review its species, age, diet, and minimum enclosure information. Contact us if you have questions about a listing.

02

Prepare the enclosure

Have a complete, species-appropriate enclosure ready. Include a clear photo of the full setup with your application.

03

Submit an application

Tell us how to reach you and which animal you are interested in. Save the private tracking code shown after submission.

04

Wait for review

We review applications individually and may contact you using the email address you provide.
