Permits & Licenses

ScaleWise Education is committed to complying with applicable Arizona and federal laws governing animal ownership, transportation, and educational presentations. Our animal ambassadors are legally acquired, and we maintain bills of sale and other applicable documentation to establish their lawful acquisition.

Legally acquired, non-restricted wildlife may be possessed and transported in accordance with applicable Arizona Game and Fish Department regulations. Wildlife that requires additional authorization is maintained and used in accordance with the applicable licensing requirements. Any Arizona-native wildlife collected or rescued in Arizona that falls under applicable wildlife holding license requirements is documented and included under the appropriate authorization.

ScaleWise Education operates through mobile educational presentations. Animals are transported securely and brought out only when needed for educational purposes, with appropriate attention to animal welfare and public safety.

Relevant Arizona authorities include A.R.S. § 17-306 (possession and transportation of live wildlife), A.R.S. § 17-101(24) (definition of wildlife, including reptiles), and Arizona Administrative Code, Title 12, Chapter 4, including R12-4-409 (special license requirements), R12-4-417 (wildlife holding license), and R12-4-428 (captivity standards).

Depending on the species and activity, applicable federal authorities may include the Endangered Species Act, 16 U.S.C. § 1538, and the Lacey Act, 16 U.S.C. § 3372.

Animal Transportation and Display Rules

ScaleWise Education prioritizes the safety, comfort, and well-being of all animals during transportation and educational programs. The following rules are followed whenever animals are transported or displayed.

Transportation

  • Secure Containers: Animals are transported in secure, escape-resistant containers appropriate for their species and size.
  • Temperature Control: Appropriate temperatures are maintained during transportation. Heating equipment is used when necessary and monitored to prevent overheating or burns.
  • Species-Specific Needs: Transportation conditions are adjusted to accommodate each animal's individual needs, including ventilation, moisture, and environmental conditions.
  • Minimizing Stress: Unnecessary handling and disturbances are avoided during transportation.
  • Travel Duration: Travel times are kept as short as reasonably possible. Food and water are provided according to the animal's needs and the duration of travel.
  • Monitoring: Animals are checked before and after transportation and monitored during travel when appropriate.
  • Vehicle Safety: Animals are never left unattended in vehicles, particularly during extreme temperatures.

Educational Display

  • As-Needed Display: Animals are brought out only when they are needed for a specific portion of an educational program.
  • Secure Storage: When an animal is not being presented, it is returned to its secure transportation container.
  • Direct Supervision: Animals remain under direct supervision whenever outside their transportation containers.
  • Limited Handling: Animal handling is kept to what is necessary for the educational presentation.
  • Animal Welfare: If an animal displays signs of stress or discomfort, it will be returned to its container and removed from the presentation.
  • Public Interaction: Any interaction between attendees and animals is controlled and supervised by the ScaleWise Education presenter.

ScaleWise Education reserves the right to remove an animal from a presentation at any time if its safety or well-being may be compromised.
