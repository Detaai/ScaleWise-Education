Our Programs

ScaleWise Education offers engaging reptile presentations for schools, homeschool groups, libraries, organizations, and private groups. Choose from three educational programs, each approximately 60 minutes long and featuring live reptiles.

Snake Identification & Bite Safety

Learn how to identify snakes, distinguish potentially dangerous species, understand common snake myths, and respond appropriately to a snakebite.

Reptile Husbandry

Learn about responsible reptile ownership, habitat requirements, proper care, and the needs of reptiles kept as pets.

Reptiles Native to Mohave County

Discover the native reptiles of our region, their habitats, their roles in the local ecosystem, and how to coexist safely with them.

Program Pricing

Pricing is based on the type of organization booking the presentation, not on which program you choose. All three programs are approximately 60 minutes and include live reptiles.
